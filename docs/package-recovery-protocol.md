# Locked synthetic package-pickup recovery protocol

Locked  before new recovery simulation. This is a separate, synthetic contextual recovery task. It tests whether an independently reward-trained teacher's preferred recovery action, conveyed by measured DN spikes, improves the speed of a student's own reward-plus-imitation learning. No physical robot, sequential planning, or safety claim follows.

## Task and observation

One failed pickup produces one immediate recovery attempt. Four hidden causes occur equally often in training and evaluation: misplaced grasp (M0), unstable orientation (M1), obstructed pickup (M2), and failed transfer (M3). The controller never sees cause. A hypothetical post-failure force/alignment/slip check emits one of three *noisy categorical signatures*: C0 alignment offset, C1 slip/rotation, C2 high resistance. Conditional probabilities `P(C0,C1,C2 | cause)` are M0 `(0.70,0.20,0.10)`, M1 `(0.20,0.70,0.10)`, M2 `(0.10,0.10,0.80)`, M3 `(0.20,0.60,0.20)`. This categorical sensor is an engineered synthetic abstraction; raw force traces are not simulated. Because every cause can emit every signature, the cue does not reveal the cause or correct recovery.

Recovery actions are A0 regrip/reposition (2 seconds), A1 rotate/stabilize (4 seconds), A2 clear obstruction and request an assisted reset (8 seconds plus 0.20 escalation cost). One Bernoulli success is sampled per action and case before any arm acts; only the selected action's outcome is revealed to its controller. `P(success | M0..M3, A0/A1/A2)` is M0 `(0.78,0.35,0.20)`, M1 `(0.32,0.78,0.22)`, M2 `(0.12,0.15,0.82)`, M3 `(0.36,0.62,0.30)`. Reward is `+1` for success or `-0.4` for a failed attempt, minus `0.03 × delay seconds`, minus `0.20` for A2 escalation. Log success, failed attempts, delay, escalation, and total net reward separately. The recovery terminates after that attempt; the existing contextual bandit therefore needs no new sequential controller. From the fixed distributions, the best *expected* action conditional on C0/C1/C2 is A0/A1/A2; this is used only to score source quality, never given to an agent. Expected reward and realized success are separately reported. Equal action count and the action-specific costs make this a stochastic recovery decision, not deterministic RPS with new names.

Each fly sees only C0/C1/C2 via its existing `.slice(2)` VPN lane at 150 Hz. The three pooled MBON rates are standardized with the existing frozen RPS feature mean/std. The VPN/KC/MBON simulation and KC→MBON multipliers are unchanged and frozen. An analytical three-MBON nearest-centroid classifier is fitted on six reward-free calibration recordings per cue and evaluated on six disjoint recordings per cue; it is never installed in a policy.

## Seeds, schedule, and learning

Initial teacher/student neural seeds: **14001/14002**. One independent confirmation, only after every initial gate passes: **14003/14004**. A search of existing docs/runners found no earlier use of these identities. Initial/confirmation training-case RNG seeds: **2026110811/2026110813**; evaluation-case seeds: **2026110812/2026110814**; generic-message seeds: **2026110815/2026110816**. Each stage has **144** training cases (36 per hidden cause, shuffled once) and **36** independent held-out cases (9 per cause, independently shuffled), with separately sampled cues and potential outcomes. Case order, neural/action keys, initial weights, and potential outcomes are fixed across the five student arms. Evaluation cases are never used for weight updates or source selection.

Fly A uses the existing three-MBON `RpsContextualBandit`: selected-action reward prediction at alpha **0.03**, epsilon **0.20**, zero initial Q, and frozen KC→MBON weights. It trains once for **144** own-reward attempts, then is frozen. Its three-action preference for each student training case's *public cue* is recorded from actual frozen MBON simulation, with no hidden cause or outcome input. The student uses the existing VPN-routed `ChallengeCueHeadBandit` plus unchanged post-reward softmax imitation (rate **0.03**) of a decoded preference. Its own-reward selected-action update remains **0.03**, epsilon **0.20**, and its synaptic weights remain frozen. No teacher/student weights are copied.

Teacher: 400 ms cycle, cue 0–240, ordinary count reset 50, choice/outcome 240, dopamine/readout update 340. Student: fresh student neural state each case, cue 0–240, count reset 50, own action 240, DN pulse 240–280, outcome 280, own-reward update followed by imitation 620, end 680. Prechosen target A0/A1/A2 stimulates actual DN 3141/3165 at 300 Hz using `(1,0)/(0,1)/(1,1)`. The DN spikes are counted during 240–280; only the decoded count code reaches imitation. The student acts before the message; advice affects future decisions through learning, not the current action. The receiver is an engineered measured-spike channel; it does not establish a causal role for downstream DN synapses.

Arms: trained teacher, no message, untrained zero-Q teacher, shuffled trained preference, and cue-independent generic preference. Shuffling rotates the frozen trained source table by a fixed **37-case** offset; the actual target histogram and within-cue alignment must be reported. Generic messages use the fixed generic RNG. All messaged arms get 144 DN pulses and imitation opportunities; the no-message arm gets none. Their own rewards and underlying case/outcome tape are matched. Frozen student evaluations are independent simulator instances, with teacher disconnected, epsilon off, no reward or imitation update, on identical 36 held-out cases after **0, 6, 12, 24, 48, 144** attempts. Preserve all trial rows and controller weights/integrity checks. Outcomes are sampled ex ante for matching, but only the selected outcome is revealed to an agent after its decision.

## Sequential gates and stop rules

1. **Task/observability:** distributions each sum to one; all hidden causes and actions appear; no preaction input contains hidden cause, potential outcomes, optimal action, or correct action. Enumerated cue-conditioned expected rewards must give three distinct unique best actions and each best-action advantage over second best at least **0.03**. Failure stops before simulation.
2. **Sensory:** actual VPN routing **6/6 per cue**, held-out MBON nearest-centroid accuracy **≥14/18 overall and ≥4/6 per cue**, finite features, distinct centroids, unchanged neural weights. Failure stops before teacher training.
3. **Teacher:** on matched frozen held-out cases, final mean cause-conditioned *expected net reward* ≥ **0.20**, at least **0.10** above its initial frozen value; at least **22/36** realized recoveries, at least **4** selections of each action, changed controller weights and unchanged KC→MBON weights. Its frozen source preference must match the analytical cue-conditioned expected-best action on **≥9/12** of the first 12 independent probes per cue and use all three actions. Failure stops before receiver/student simulation. These reference actions are analysis-only.
4. **Receiver:** six matched probes per cue under no pulse and all three codes (**72** probes); intended DN spikes >0 and off-target counts zero, code decoding **6/6** per cue/code, no-message DN counts zero, VPN routing **6/6**, exact paired pre-pulse MBON features, unchanged weights. Failure stops.
5. **Independent student and control validity:** after 144 no-message reward attempts, frozen expected net reward ≥ **0.20** and ≥ **0.10** above its initial value, ≥ **22/36** realized recoveries, and all actions used ≥4 times. A decoded synthetic target must increase its score margin while disabled imitation changes no weights. Shuffled and generic message tapes must each contain all three actions and match the cue-conditioned expected-best action on **≤50%** of the 144 case cues. If any gate fails, stop without resampling or altering a control. Record any completed control arms even on a validity stop.
6. **Teaching comparison:** primary metric is mean frozen expected net reward at attempts **6/12/24/48**. The trained arm must exceed *each* control by **≥0.06** reward units, first reach **0.20** expected net reward by attempt 48 at an earlier sampled checkpoint than every control, and finish with expected net reward **≥0.20** and **≥22/36** realized recoveries. Equal checkpoint timing fails. Report frozen success, failed attempts, delay, escalation, reward, all controller updates, and message fidelity as secondary measurements. Any failure stops. Only a complete initial pass permits one independent confirmation with the locked second pair and same gates.
