# Locked practical-transfer protocol: synthetic package recovery

**Locked  before new neural simulation or reward training.** This is one new, isolated experimental controller architecture prompted by the [read-only diagnosis](practical-transfer-controller-diagnosis.md). The older package-recovery failures and RPS results remain unchanged. No result from this protocol may be used to tune the controller, seeds, thresholds, or controls.

## Task and observations

Reuse **exactly** the four equiprobable hidden causes, three noisy observable sensor signatures, three action success-probability rows, and reward/cost rule in [the original protocol](package-recovery-protocol.md). A selected attempt yields success `y∈{0,1}` and net reward `r=(y?1:-0.4)-0.03×delay[a]-0.20×1[a=2]`. The teacher and student cannot observe hidden cause, success probabilities, unselected outcomes, or the analytical best action. Every case's outcomes are sampled before arms for matching but only the chosen outcome and reward reach a controller. The task is synthetic, one-step, and uses an engineered categorical sensor, not a robot.

Each fly's observable signature drives its existing VPN lane at 150 Hz for 0–240 ms, with ordinary count reset at 50 ms. Its three pooled MBON rates become `f_j=(log(1+rate_j)-fixed_RPS_mean_j)/fixed_RPS_std_j`. For each neural seed, six **reward-free** calibration probes per signature fit three Euclidean MBON centroids; six disjoint probes per signature check them. The fixed nearest centroid then routes each training/evaluation feature to one of three heads. Calibration uses the *observable signature identity* only, not hidden causes, rewards, outcomes, or optimal actions. This installed sensory decoder is an engineered addition and may be wrong; it is not the analytical best-action decoder from previous reports. All training decisions use measured MBON features, never the raw signature directly. Real-wiring VPN/KC/MBON dynamics are simulated; KC→MBON plastic multipliers remain frozen at one.

## One new controller

For each decoded signature `c` and recovery action `a`, keep independent Beta success parameters `α_ca=1`, `β_ca=1`, and an integer `n_ca=0` counting **own selected-action outcomes**. Known fixed action costs are `k=[0.06,0.12,0.44]`; no success probability is supplied. A selected own outcome `y` updates only its chosen cell: `α_ca+=y`, `β_ca+=1-y`, `n_ca+=1`. Its recorded net reward must agree exactly with that outcome and cost. During training, while any action in the current decoded cue has `n<6`, choose an action with the smallest `n` (lowest action index on ties). This is a fixed, cue-conditional coverage phase, independent of outcome, hidden cause, and teacher message. Thereafter choose the lowest-index maximizer of

`U_ca = 1.4×min(1, μ_ca+σ_ca)-0.4-k_a`, where `μ=α/(α+β)` and `σ²=αβ/[(α+β)²(α+β+1)]`.

Frozen evaluation uses only `Q_ca=1.4 μ_ca-0.4-k_a`, lowest-index argmax, with no exploration or updates. The fixed coefficient of **one posterior standard deviation**, uniform Beta prior, six own observations per cue/action, and all costs are locked. This is a heuristic Bayesian outcome model, not a calibrated posterior for drifting neural features. The coverage phase and separate heads are a *joint architecture change*; their individual causal contributions cannot be separated by this study. The matched historical LinUCB algorithm (ridge 1, bonus 0.5) is replayed on the **same teacher neural feature/case tapes** as a descriptive sample-efficiency comparator, with its own independent state and selected-action rewards. It receives no privileged information. No LinUCB gate is changed retrospectively.

## Teacher, message, and student

Fly A trains for **144** own-reward cases (36 per hidden cause, shuffled once) and is then frozen. It produces a preference on each student's public sensory signature by its own new controller's frozen MBON-driven `Q` argmax on a separate frozen neural probe. The teacher receives no student reward or hidden cause. A source probe set has 12 independent reward-free trials per signature. Before student training, its preferred action must equal the analysis-only cue-conditioned best action on ≥9/12 per signature and use all three actions. This gate conditions whether a useful source exists; it does not install an oracle.

Student architecture is identical to teacher architecture. It chooses its action **before** a DN message, receives its own selected recovery outcome and cost, updates its own `α/β/n`, and then applies a fixed auxiliary imitation update: add **0.25** to `α_c,decoded_action` for the current *student-decoded* cue. Advice never increments `n`, counts as an own observed recovery, or directly selects a played action. This fractional pseudo-success is an **engineered hybrid imitation objective**, not dopamine KC→MBON plasticity; it can be harmful if advice is wrong. Source preference is encoded as DNge175 `(3141,3165)` drive `(1,0)/(0,1)/(1,1)` at 300 Hz during **240–280 ms** of the existing 680 ms post-sensory schedule. The receiver counts actual spikes in a separate buffer, decodes only those counts, and passes the decoded action to imitation at 620 ms. No intended action label enters the student update. Student neural state resets each case, and Fly A is not updated during student training.

The five student arms are **trained**, **alone**, **untrained**, **shuffled**, and **generic**. Four messaged arms each have 144 DN pulses and possible imitation steps; alone has none. Untrained Fly A has its initial uniform Beta state and gives its own frozen MBON-driven preference (ties choose the lowest action). Shuffled advice uses the trained 144-preference tape rotated by 37 case positions, preserving its global action counts. Generic advice uses a fixed cue-independent random action tape. No control generator reads outcome or best-action labels. All student arms have identical initial controller/neural state, case order, potential outcomes, keyed neural streams, and held-out evaluations. Record alignment of control tapes; do not resample a control if it happens to be useful. Student sensory calibration is shared across its arms and fitted before reward training.

These identities were searched in existing docs/tools before locking. Each training tape has 144 cases (36 per cause); each independent frozen evaluation tape has 36 cases (9 per cause), with 3 potential Bernoulli outcomes sampled per case. Teacher and student have different tapes. Neural keys are `neuralSeed×10^7 + (phaseOffset+caseIndex)×1000 + 1`, with phaseOffset `0` training, `900` evaluation, `1800` source, `2700` sensory calibration. Each neural probe starts from reset state and uses `rng(key)`; arms share each student training/evaluation key. The source uses the teacher's separate frozen probe stream. No case or outcome RNG is used by a controller.

Teacher and student frozen checkpoints: **0, 24, 48, 96, 144** own-reward training cases for independent learning. Student comparison additionally records **6 and 12**. At each checkpoint, evaluate an independent copied frozen controller on identical 36 held-out cases with fresh reset neural probes, no DN message, no exploration, no learning, no imitation, and unchanged KC→MBON weights. Report realized success/failures, delay, escalation, selected action counts, per-cue choices, and hidden-cause-conditioned expected net reward **for analysis only**. Primary student early metric is the mean frozen expected net reward at **24/48/96** cases, not online training reward. Also report first sampled checkpoint reaching +0.20, final disconnected result, training failures/costs, and message fidelity. The matched LinUCB teacher comparator uses the same frozen checkpoints and features, but its old historical gates are not reapplied to new cases.

## Sequential validity and success gates

1. **Correctness before neural run:** the no-simulation unit suite verifies independent cells, six-per-cell coverage, selected-outcome-only updates, cost-consistent reward, posterior uncertainty decrease after balanced evidence, frozen no-exploration/no-change behavior, snapshot restoration, and imitation from decoded action only. The implementation may not accept hidden cause, unchosen outcomes, or optimal-action labels. Failure stops before neural simulation.
2. **Task/sensory:** original distribution and reward tables are unchanged; analytical cue-best actions remain A0/A1/A2 with ≥0.03 gap each. For **each selected teacher/student seed before training**, calibration and held-out probes are disjoint, VPN routing is 6/6 per cue, MBON nearest-centroid recognition ≥14/18 overall and ≥4/6 per cue, features finite, and neural weights unchanged. Failure stops with no seed replacement.
3. **Teacher independent learning:** final frozen expected reward ≥**+0.20** and gain ≥**+0.10** above its own checkpoint 0; ≥**22/36** actual successes; each recovery action chosen ≥**4** times; C2 clearing chosen on ≥**60%** of held-out C2 cases; controller changed and KC→MBON weights did not. Source preference must meet the 9/12-per-cue rule above. Failure stops before DN or student training. Record matched LinUCB curves and training failures, but do not select a source based on that comparator.
4. **Receiver:** six matched probes per cue under no pulse and each of the three action codes. All **72** probes must have exact paired pre-pulse MBON features and decoded cue, VPN cue routing 6/6, and unchanged weights. No-message DN counts must be zero; each forced code must produce positive intended and zero off-target spikes and decode 6/6 per cue/code. Failure stops.
5. **Independent student:** the alone arm after 144 own rewards must reach ≥**+0.20** frozen expected reward, gain ≥**+0.10**, ≥**22/36** successes, and ≥**4** frozen selections of each action. All arms must have matched initial states, 144 own-reward updates, equal case/feature streams, and frozen integrity. A synthetic decoded-message unit check must change its nominated action value; disabled imitation must not. Failure stops before comparison.
6. **Teacher-specific pilot:** trained advice must exceed **each** alone, untrained, shuffled, and generic arm by ≥**+0.04 expected-reward units** on the 24/48/96 early mean, first reach +0.20 no later than 96 at an **earlier sampled checkpoint** than every control, and finish at ≥+0.20 expected reward with ≥22/36 successes and ≥4 choices per action. All forced messages must decode from measured DN spikes, and frozen evaluation must be disconnected. Any failed gate is a valid negative result; do not retune or rerun. Only a complete initial pass permits the fixed independent confirmation, with the **same** gates and second identities above.

Write each started stage once to `data/practical_transfer_{initial,confirmation}.json` with this protocol's SHA-256, trial-level rows, snapshots, and gate outcomes. No artifact is overwritten. A pass would establish a small synthetic hybrid teacher-specific learning-speed result, not real warehouse deployment, biological teaching, or neural transmission superiority over digital messaging.
