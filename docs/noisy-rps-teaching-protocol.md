# Locked noisy Strategy A teacher-specific early-learning test

**Question.** After 24 reward-learning rounds against the existing stochastic previous-move opponent, does advice from a reward-trained Fly A improve Fly B's early *disconnected* RPS policy more than silence, causally time-shuffled trained advice, or an untrained Fly A? This is separate from the earlier one-seed two-arm demo pilot and all historical noisy-receiver gates. No demo or learning-rule changes are authorized by this protocol.

**Fixed task and identities.** Existing Strategy A, `noise=0.3`: conditional on the actually observed previous opponent move, the base next move has probability 0.8 and each alternative 0.1. The controller sees only the previous move through the unchanged VPN→KC→MBON pathway and VPN-derived cue head; it never sees the opponent's random draw, hidden next move, or correct action. Fixed teacher **931**, trained from its own rewards for 12×24 rounds on opponent seeds **41000–41011** with the existing three-MBON contextual bandit and fixed feature calibration. Its frozen 72-round result on **51000–51002** must exactly reproduce the archived **50/72** pilot before student training. Student seeds **12341, 12342, 12343** were checked against existing research docs and runners before any new run. All four arms for each student begin with identical zero cue-head weights, the same student neural seed, keyed neural/action random streams, and the same 24 opponent rounds from seed **41000**. The pilot seed 10931 is excluded.

**Four arms.** `trained`: Fly A's frozen MBON-driven Q argmax for the current observed cue, encoded at 300 Hz on DNge175 neurons 3141/3165 and decoded from actual 240–280 ms spike counts. `alone`: no message or imitation, ordinary own-reward update. `untrained`: identical frozen teacher architecture/network with zero trained readout, same current cue and DN path. `shuffled`: the trained teacher is simulated on the same cue schedule, but its preference delivered from a fixed seeded shuffle **of previously generated messages only**; on the first cue-bearing round the current preference is used, thereafter the shuffle samples from the accumulated past preferences and appends the current one *after* the decision. This causal shuffle avoids using future opponent moves while matching one delivered neural message per cue-bearing round. It may not preserve exact action-symbol counts; report them. No message or imitation on the first cue-free round. All delivered messages use the same measured-spike decoder and the unchanged `.03` imitation update after Fly B's own `.03` selected-action reward update. Teacher weights remain frozen, KC→MBON multipliers remain one, and each student acts before the opponent's next move is sampled.

**Frozen checks and gate.** Evaluate independent frozen controller/network copies after **0, 12, and 24** training rounds. Every arm/seed uses opponent seeds **51000–51002** and matching keyed neural streams, 72 rounds per checkpoint, with teacher disconnected, exploration disabled, and all learning disabled. Store trial rows, action/reward, cue, decoded DN counts during training, controller weight integrity, and matched opponent schedules. The **primary early metric is frozen wins at round 12**. A teacher-specific pass requires, for **each of all three predetermined students**, trained advice to exceed the **strongest of the three controls by at least 4/72 wins at round 12**. Also require exact teacher replay, identical initial frozen scores and opponent sequences, 100% decoding on every delivered message, no DN activity or imitation in silence/frozen evaluations, and unchanged teacher and KC→MBON weights. Round-24 final wins are reported separately and cannot rescue an early failure. Any failed validity prerequisite stops the run; any failed performance gate is preserved without tuning or seed replacement. One focused correctness check precedes one locked run. No confirmation follows automatically.

**Control validity.** At least **6 of 23** cue-bearing shuffled targets must differ from the contemporaneous trained target; otherwise stop because the shuffled arm did not materially disrupt the message. The trained source tape must reproduce all 24 saved pilot preferences, including the first `null`. Message symbol counts and actual DN counts are recorded for every advice arm.

**Interpretation.** A pass on these three seeds would support a narrow noisy-Strategy-A early learning effect through the engineered hybrid neural channel; it would not establish biological teaching or an advantage over direct digital messages. A failure keeps this noisy opponent an exploratory demo challenge, not validated teacher-specific research evidence.
