# Two-cue channel interference check

One diagnostic seed (11), 24 balanced cue rounds per condition. Fly B was trained for 10 episodes on each mapping, then its weights were frozen. Cue-only, signal-only, and combined conditions used identical starting weights and cue order. The message bits are the saved, actual trained Fly A neuron 2132 bits from the first post-switch episode of the earlier pilot. Cue targets remain VPN lanes 0/2; message targets remain the first eight VPNs in lanes 0/1. The correct actions in this check use the reversed mapping (cue 0 -> action 2; cue 1 -> action 0). No feedback or synaptic updates occurred during these condition comparisons.

| Setting | Message | Cue | Cue only | Signal only | Cue + message | Combined cue 0 | Combined cue 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original | 300 Hz, 120-240 ms | 0-240 ms | 22/24 | 10/24 | 8/24 | 1/12 | 7/12 |
| Weaker | 150 Hz, 120-240 ms | 0-240 ms | 22/24 | 15/24 | 13/24 | 6/12 | 7/12 |
| Shorter | 300 Hz, 180-240 ms | 0-240 ms | 22/24 | 14/24 | 15/24 | 6/12 | 9/12 |
| No direct overlap | 300 Hz, 180-240 ms | 0-180 ms | 23/24 | 14/24 | 17/24 | 8/12 | 9/12 |

Cue-only KC active counts averaged 1.3 for cue 0 and 1.9-2.1 for cue 1. Combined counts were 57.9/70.8 with the original setting and still 24.7/43.9 with no direct overlap. For the original setting, cue-only MBON rates (actions 0/2) were 0.10/0.26 for cue 0 and 1.31/0.73 for cue 1. Combined rates became 2.02/1.86 and 3.04/2.97, respectively: the original cue-linked separation was largely overwhelmed. With no direct overlap, the combined rates were 0.61/0.67 and 1.76/1.52. The natural-bit combined condition selected action 0 on 18/24 rounds originally and 13/24 with no direct overlap. Message value 0 versus 1 still changed the action on 8/24 matched trials with no direct overlap; their KC active sets differed by 45.7 neurons per matched round on average, and the three MBON rates differed by Euclidean distance 0.63. Distinguishable activity alone was insufficient to preserve the cue decision.

The earlier teaching result of 1/72 correct for cue 1 is a training outcome. Rechecking the first 24 teaching rounds across seeds 101-103, trained-teacher Fly B got 1/36 cue-1 trials correct and chose the wrong action 2 on 34/36; no-teacher Fly B got 20/36 correct. Cue-1 KC active counts averaged 30.7 versus 1.9, and MBON action-0/action-2 rates averaged 0.71/1.38 versus 0.79/0.86. This supports a message-induced action-2 bias in that experiment. The frozen check isolates acute interference and is not a reproduction of the 72-round training result.

The best setting (no direct overlap) was also tested with the existing balanced two-label known-signal diagnostic: 12 training episodes and one 24-round held-out episode on seed 11. Accuracy was 1/24 before training, 13/24 with the correct signal, 4/24 disconnected, 9/24 shuffled, and 4/24 for the measured signal-independent policy. The correct-signal result is only 54.2%, near the relevant 50% binary-label chance level; this short run does not establish reliable decoding. The signal-independent policy is also reported because the agent can choose a third action.

**Decision:** The three targeted changes reduced acute interference but none preserved cue responses well enough to justify teaching trials. No channel change was adopted, and no three-seed teaching comparison was run. The likely immediate limitation is broad activation from the message VPNs into KCs and MBONs, persisting even after the cue drive ends. The next smallest experiment is a paired single-seed test with a still briefer, lower-amplitude message pulse placed after the cue, checking cue-only versus combined action agreement and known-signal decoding before any teaching. This is a recommendation, not an implemented fourth setting.

Reproduce the frozen interference check with `node tools/two_cue_transfer.mjs interference 11 02 lanes02 150` and the short decoder check with `node tools/calibrate_rps_channel.mjs quick`. Both commands refuse to overwrite their existing results; move the outputs aside first if rerunning. Raw measurements: `data/two_cue_interference_02_11_lanes02.json` and `data/rps_channel_quick_eight_60ms_300hz.json`.
