# Practical transfer: archived recovery bottleneck (read-only, )

This diagnosis uses the saved synthetic package-recovery trials in `data/package_recovery_initial.json`, `data/package_recovery_uncertainty_initial.json`, and `data/package_recovery_learnability.json`. It does not revise their failed gates. The task supplies one noisy categorical sensor signature through simulated VPN/KC/MBON activity; the hidden failure cause, unchosen outcomes, and correct action are unavailable to the learner.

| Observation from the archives | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| The original reward-trained contextual bandit ended at +0.1943 expected reward, 22/36 realized recoveries, and 0 frozen clearing actions. It tried clearing 12 times on high-resistance C2 during training; the selected feedback averaged -0.257. | Clearing was sampled, but the finite selected feedback made its learned value unfavorable. No update-code error was found by replaying the saved equations. |
| The separate LinUCB teacher run had fewer training failures (68 vs 82) but missed three locked final gates, including 7/12 C2 clearing choices. | Its fixed linear uncertainty bonus helped online exploration but did not establish a reliable frozen teacher. |
| In the two-seed learnability test, direct-signature and MBON-feature LinUCB learners all selected clearing zero times at final frozen evaluation. They received only 4, 4, 1, and 1 C2-clearing reward samples, respectively; sampled mean rewards were -0.14, -0.14, -0.84, and -0.84. | Undercoverage and noisy selected-action feedback occurred even without neural feature loss. A nonzero exploration probability or uncertainty bonus did not guarantee finite C2-clearing coverage. |
| The fixed analytical MBON signature decoder recognized 35/36 and 32/36 held-out signatures on the two new neural seeds. The raw-signature and decoder-based expected-reward references exceeded the learned policies on their respective tapes. | Useful sensory information reached MBONs, though trial variability remained. These references used labels for analysis and were not installed in the old controllers. |

**Specific deficiency to test:** the earlier learners did not ensure a useful number of *cue-conditional* clearing outcomes before committing to a frozen policy. A shared linear action-value surface also allowed rewards from other cues to influence C2, but its causal contribution has not been isolated. Stochastic feedback, feature variability, controller structure, and trial order remain possible joint causes. The next architecture therefore combines a fixed minimum number of outcome samples per decoded cue/action with separate Bernoulli outcome estimates for each cue/action. This tests a package, not an isolated causal effect of any one component. The model's known fixed delay/escalation costs convert estimated success probabilities to net values. It will use only observed MBON activity and selected-action success/failure for decisions and updates; a reward-free decoder may use the *observable sensor signature* during calibration, never hidden cause, reward, or best-action labels.

The strongest established teaching evidence remains the separately audited hybrid Strategy A/B RPS action-preference result. The [connectome ablation](connectome-contribution-ablation-report.md) found measured DN and direct digital delivery identical in its fixed RPS comparison. This recovery branch cannot be presented as a real robot result or a distinct biological-connectome advantage.
