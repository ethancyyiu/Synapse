# Prospective practical-transfer redesign: independent recovery learning

**Design locked ; no simulation or training has been run under this protocol.** This is a new synthetic, one-attempt package-pickup recovery benchmark. It is separate from the failed [original recovery](package-recovery-report.md), [LinUCB comparison](package-recovery-uncertainty-report.md), [raw-versus-neural learnability](package-recovery-learnability-report.md), [practical transfer](practical-transfer-report.md), and [sensory validation](package-recovery-sensory-validation-report.md) studies. Their gates and artifacts remain unchanged. The independently audited [Strategy A](action-preference-independent-audit.md) and [Strategy B](action-preference-strategy-b-independent-audit.md) RPS results remain the demonstrated teaching evidence; this design cannot inherit their success.

## Hypothesis and reason for the redesign

The earlier synthetic recovery controllers saw only a noisy categorical signature. The raw-observable LinUCB arms received just **4 and 1** selected C2/clearing rewards in the two-seed learnability test and selected clearing zero times in frozen evaluation. The later per-cue Beta controller forced six observations per decoded cue/action but the installed three-pooled-MBON centroid routed **30/47** C0 training inputs to the C2 head; it failed its own teacher gate. Independent reward-free validation found separated VPN signals and class-related KC/MBON responses, but a fresh pooled-centroid fit missed the C2 recognition floor on one stream. These observations support *both* sparse action/context feedback and fragile hard cue routing as concrete risks. They do not prove a unique causal explanation or that individual MBONs are superior.

**New hypothesis:** A continuous, observable three-sensor task with sufficient controlled action coverage, a reward-only uncertainty-aware controller, and direct individual-MBON features can support independent recovery learning without an external hard cue decoder. This is a joint new benchmark, not an isolated ablation of earlier methods. It may fail at raw learnability, sensory representation, or neural learning; each failure stops the chain.

## Fixed synthetic task

Each episode starts after a failed package pickup and ends after **one** selected recovery attempt. Immediately before acting, the agent observes three dimensionless diagnostics in `[0,1]`: lateral grasp offset `o` (vision), package slip/rotation `s` (motion/gripper), and obstruction resistance `b` (force). The simulator supplies these values directly to the raw baseline and only through the neural sensory drive to the neural agent. They are synthetic telemetry, not recorded robot signals, latent cause labels, action answers, or future outcomes. The agent sees no episode index, reward-model coefficients, other agents' parameters, or unchosen outcome.

Actions have distinct operational meanings: `A0` regrip/reposition (2 seconds); `A1` stabilize/rotate (4 seconds); `A2` clear obstruction and request an assisted reset (7 seconds and 0.10 escalation cost). The fixed action costs are `k=[0.06,0.12,0.31]`, comprising `0.03 × delay seconds` plus A2 escalation. A selected action succeeds with one Bernoulli draw whose probability depends smoothly on *all* observed diagnostics:

```
sigmoid(z) = 1/(1+exp(-z))
p0(o,s,b) = sigmoid(1.6 + 1.2o - 1.5s - 2.0b)
p1(o,s,b) = sigmoid(1.3 - 0.6o + 2.0s - 1.7b)
p2(o,s,b) = sigmoid(0.4 - 0.3o - 0.5s + 3.3b)
r(a,y) = (y ? 1 : -0.4) - k[a],  y ~ Bernoulli(pa)
E[r | o,s,b,a] = 1.4pa(o,s,b) - 0.4 - k[a]
```

The continuous inputs and overlapping success probabilities make this a stochastic decision surface, not a three-state RPS lookup table. The equations and their coefficients are **simulator assumptions**, unknown to the learner. The expected-value oracle may use them only for analytical task validity and postdecision scoring; no oracle action or probability is an input to Fly A. Failures, elapsed delay, escalation, realized net reward, and model-expected net reward are reported separately. There is no sequential navigation, unseen failure cause, or physical-robot claim.

## Fixed cases and identities

The three binary half-ranges of `(o,s,b)` define **eight octants solely for case balancing and diagnostic reporting**, not a policy input or a hand-coded action label. Within each assigned octant, each continuous coordinate is sampled independently and uniformly. Every evaluation triple is newly sampled; no training case is repeated. A 5×5×5 fixed midpoint grid across the full cube is used only for the *pre-training task-validity gate* below.

| Purpose | Neural identity / stream | Case and outcome stream | Size |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Reward-free feature scaling | 19101 | 20261109115 | 96 probes, 12/octant |
| Initial matched raw/neural teacher | 19102 | train 20261109111; evaluation 20261109112; sensory check 20261109116; controller 20261109118 | 480 training; 120 held-out; 96 sensory-check probes |
| Conditional independent teacher replication | 19103 | train 20261109113; evaluation 20261109114; sensory check 20261109117; controller 20261109119 | same |

These identifiers were checked against existing documentation and runners before any new data collection. The real connectome wiring is not a new biological sample: the neural identities primarily define independent keyed neural random streams and simulator states. The raw and neural arms within an identity share the **same** continuous cases, case order, pre-sampled potential Bernoulli outcomes, action-independent costs, training budget, and held-out tape. Only the selected action's sampled outcome is revealed after its decision. Separate controller and neural RNG streams are fixed from the listed seeds, with per-case keyed neural streams; no random stream is reseeded after seeing an outcome. Each one-attempt episode resets neural state before cue stimulation.

Use the repository's existing `rng(seed)` generator. For each case tape, shuffle the specified octant-label multiset with seeded Fisher–Yates, then, in that order, draw the three within-octant uniform coordinates followed by the three Bernoulli potential outcomes in action order A0/A1/A2. The coverage tape shuffles octants separately within each 24-case block, with three consecutive independent draws per octant; its outcome draws follow each case's coordinates. Save each tape before either matched arm acts. Raw and neural controller draws use **separate RNG instances initialized to the same listed controller seed**, so each consumes three standard-normal draws per free training decision regardless of chosen action; generate each from two uniforms using `sqrt(-2 ln(max(u1,2^-32))) cos(2πu2)`. No controller draws occur in forced coverage or frozen evaluation. Neural probe RNG keys are `neuralSeed×10^7 + phase×10^6 + caseIndex×1000 + 1`, with phase 0 calibration, 1 sensory check, 2 training, and 3 frozen evaluation; use independent simulator instances for checkpoints. Record effective keys and reject overlap **across distinct probes** before use; frozen checkpoints intentionally reuse the same evaluation keys for matched comparison.

Training has **480** cases: the first **288** are twelve 24-case coverage blocks, each containing three independent continuous cases in each of the eight octants. The octant order is seeded and fixed. The forced action order cycles through all three actions within each octant triple, rotated by block number; thus every octant/action obtains **12 selected outcomes**, regardless of reward or features. The agent is not told the octant: this is an external, label-free *training exploration schedule* only, identical in raw and neural arms. The remaining **192** cases have 24 independently drawn cases/octant in a seeded order and use the controller's stochastic exploration. Evaluation has **120** new cases, 15/octant, with independent outcome draws. This budget is anchored to 8 observable regions × 3 actions × 12 outcomes = 288 guaranteed initial feedback samples, versus the earlier 1–4 C2/clearing samples; the 192 later cases test whether generalization beyond forced coverage occurs. Twelve Bernoulli observations still have substantial uncertainty (worst-case standard error about 0.144 per cell), so passing is not presumed.

## Sensory representation: one fixed choice

| Candidate | Strength | Limitation / decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raw `(o,s,b)` | Observable, compact upper reference; bypasses neural variability. | **Required non-neural learnability baseline**, not the fly's sensory input. |
| Three pooled MBON rates | Low dimension and existing measurement. | A hard centroid on these features failed one independent sensory gate and misrouted the prior teacher; the rates may still be useful, but this protocol does not reinstall that decoder. |
| 42 individual action-MBON counts | Retains within-group activity that pooling removes and needs no hand-decoded cue. | **Chosen prospective neural features.** Greater dimension and neural noise may hurt learning; prior recordings did not validate an advantage, so the sensory and neural-learning gates below are mandatory. |

For both neural identities, simultaneously stimulate the existing VPN lanes `laneVpn[0..2].slice(2)` from **0–240 ms** at fixed rates `40+110o`, `40+110s`, and `40+110b` Hz, respectively. Reset ordinary spike counts at 50 ms and read all **42 individual MBON post-reset counts at 240 ms**. Use the existing real connectome, simulator dynamics, and reset behavior; freeze KC→MBON multipliers at one. No hidden simulator variable or best action sets a drive. This graded three-lane encoding is unvalidated: an earlier *different* simultaneous graded sensor task failed at pooled MBONs, so the sensory gate cannot be skipped.

From the 96 reward-free 19101 probes only, compute `m_j=mean(log1p(count_j))` and `d_j=max(sampleSD(log1p(count_j)),0.1)` for each of the 42 MBONs. Freeze these 84 constants before any validation or learning. Neural controller input is `x=[1, z_1/sqrt(42),...,z_42/sqrt(42)]`, `z_j=(log1p(count_j)-m_j)/d_j`. No centroid, semantic cue, supervised action decoder, or raw telemetry reaches the neural controller. The raw controller uses `x=[1,(2o-1)/sqrt(3),(2s-1)/sqrt(3),(2b-1)/sqrt(3)]`. The same learning equation below accepts either dimension; feature differences, including dimension, are reported as a limitation.

## One reward-only controller

Use **one** per-action Bayesian ridge contextual bandit in both raw and neural arms. For each action `a`, initialize `A_a=4 I_d`, `b_a=0`, with `d=4` raw or `43` neural. At current feature `x`, the posterior mean approximation and uncertainty are

```
mu_a(x) = xᵀ A_a⁻¹ b_a
sd_a(x) = 0.7 sqrt(xᵀ A_a⁻¹ x)
```

During the 192 free-exploration training cases, independently draw one `q_a ~ Normal(mu_a, sd_a²)` for each action and choose the lowest-index maximum. The 288 coverage cases use only the fixed forced action schedule. After receiving the **selected action's own** net reward `r`, update only its arm: `A_a += xxᵀ`, `b_a += r x`; do not update either unchosen arm. This is a Gaussian ridge/Thompson approximation to bounded, Bernoulli-derived rewards, **not a calibrated Bernoulli posterior**. The fixed noise scale `0.7` is the maximum standard deviation of the success-dependent reward term `1.4y`; ridge `4` regularizes the 42-dimensional neural readout. Neither value is tuned on evaluation. Frozen decisions choose the lowest-index maximum `mu_a`, with no posterior sampling, reward update, DN message, or neural plasticity. Report actual action coverage, posterior uncertainty, and parameter changes. A failure may reflect model misspecification as well as insufficient information.

This controller learns from ordinary selected-action outcomes, not the hidden probability equations or an optimal-action label. The simulator calculates expected rewards for **analysis after decisions only**. No external classifier is installed in a fly. This independent-learning protocol has no teacher message, imitation update, or student arm.

## Sequential feasibility gates and stopping rules

All criteria below apply as written; no failed criterion can be relaxed or replaced by another metric after evaluation. Initial identity 19102 is evaluated first. Identity 19103 is run **only if every initial gate passes**, as a conditional independent replication. If any stage fails, save/report all measurements collected to that point and stop without replacing seeds, extending the budget, or trying another feature/controller.

1. **Task validity, before training or neural simulation.** On the fixed 125-point midpoint grid, each of the three actions must be the unique highest-expected-reward action on at least **10%** of points; the mean best-observation policy must exceed the best constant action by at least **0.08** net reward per case. Otherwise the synthetic task is unsuitable; stop without adjusting coefficients.
2. **Raw-observable independent learning, before connectome use.** Train the 19102 raw arm on its predetermined 480 cases, then score the separate 120-case tape at frozen checkpoints **0, 288, 480**. Define `V_const` as the best *constant-action* mean model-expected reward and `V_oracle` as the mean of per-case maximum expected rewards on that tape, both analysis-only. Final raw policy must exceed its own checkpoint-0 mean by **0.05**, exceed `V_const` by **0.05**, close at least **50%** of `V_oracle - V_const`, and select every action at least **12/120** times. A nonpositive oracle gap invalidates the metric and stops. Report realized successes/costs but do not use their finite-sample fluctuation to rescue a failed expected-reward gate.
3. **Neural sensory validity, before neural reward training.** Fit only the 19101 label-blind MBON normalization above. On 96 separate reward-free training-style probes for 19102, record actual VPN counts, KC recruitment, individual MBON counts, and pooled rates. Fit one **analysis-only** ridge linear reconstruction from the 19101 neural features to the three *observable telemetry values* with penalty 4 and an unpenalized intercept; freeze it before the 19102 probes. For each telemetry dimension independently, held-out `R²` must be at least **0.40** and mean absolute error at most **0.20**; intended VPN-lane spike counts must have Pearson correlation at least **0.80** with their own requested drive, with finite nonzero variance. Verify no direct telemetry/hidden-label/controller input leakage and all neural multipliers unchanged. This decoder only measures information; it is never installed in the reward learner. Any failure stops before neural reward training.
4. **Independent neural teacher learning.** Train Fly A 19102 from the same cases/outcomes and budget as raw, using only its measured 42-MBON feature vector and own selected-action reward. At the identical frozen 0/288/480 checkpoints on the same 120 held-out cases, require final expected reward at least **0.05** above its own checkpoint 0, at least **0.05** above `V_const`, at least **50%** closure of the oracle–constant gap, and no worse than **0.05** below its matched raw final expected reward. Require at least **12/120** selections of each action, finite posterior values, changed controller parameters, and unchanged KC→MBON multipliers. Record realized success, delay, escalation, net reward, action frequencies by octant, and all training coverage; none substitutes for the primary expected-reward gate.
5. **Conditional independent replication.** Only after 19102 passes gates 1–4, repeat raw learning, reward-free sensory validation, and neural teacher learning **unchanged** on 19103 with its listed independent streams and the same numerical gates. Keep 19101 normalization and analysis-only sensory decoder frozen. An all-teacher feasibility pass requires **both** identities; one failure stops and remains a failure.

Frozen evaluation uses independent simulator/controller copies and matched held-out cases. It disables Thompson draws, forced exploration, all reward and imitation updates, and all DN messages; verify byte-equivalent controller parameters before/after and neural multipliers equal one. Prospective code, if later authorized, must save trial-level cases, features, drives/spikes, selected actions, selected outcomes, rewards/costs, expected-reward analysis, checkpoints, parameters, gates, and protocol hash to a **new write-once artifact**. No result artifact is created by this design-only session.

**Deterministic design check only:** Direct enumeration of the specified 125 midpoint triples (no outcome draws, neural simulation, controller, or training) gives unique best-action counts **28/53/44** for A0/A1/A2, best constant mean expected reward **0.5096**, and observable-oracle mean **0.6443**, a gap of **0.1348**. Thus the task-validity gate's fixed geometry passes analytically. This does not establish raw-controller learnability or any neural result; the later protocol must still check the exact same grid and stop on an implementation mismatch.

## Conditional teaching study, separately locked later

**Only an all-teacher pass would justify designing—not automatically running—a new student study.** It would keep Fly A's independently reward-trained policy frozen; its preference would be `argmax mu_a` from actual simulated MBON features. The existing three-action DN3141/3165 code would transmit that preference as measured spikes to a student with its **own** controller and reward updates. A subsequent protocol must first specify and validate how decoded preferences affect this Bayesian-ridge student; the successful RPS softmax imitation equation cannot silently be assumed equivalent. No teacher weights, raw telemetry, model probability, hidden optimal action, or intended message label would enter the student update.

The separately locked comparison would use matched initial student state, case/outcome tapes, training exposure, and frozen teacher-disconnected evaluations for **trained neural advice, silence, untrained neural advice, shuffled trained neural advice, cue-independent generic neural advice, and direct digital delivery of the identical frozen preference at the same update time**. It must predeclare receiver fidelity, source quality, independent student learnability, early expected-reward advantage over *every* valid control, realized failures/costs, final retained performance, seeds, and confirmation stop rules before student training. The direct-digital arm is necessary because the earlier RPS ablation found identical behavior for spikes and digital delivery; no neural-channel superiority may be inferred from successful transmission alone.

If this future synthetic benchmark passes, the defensible claim would be **faster retained learning of simulated recovery decisions from a reward-trained agent's action preference carried by measured neural spikes**, within the specified model and controls. It would not establish biological teaching, essential detailed DN connectivity, real warehouse performance, or deployment safety. The current design establishes **none** of those outcomes; its first required empirical gate is independent raw-observable learning, followed by neural sensory validity and independent Fly A learning.
