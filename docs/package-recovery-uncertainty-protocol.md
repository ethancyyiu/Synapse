# Locked uncertainty-aware package-recovery teacher comparison

Locked  before any new recovery training or held-out evaluation. The [original recovery protocol](package-recovery-protocol.md), its [failed result](package-recovery-report.md), all RPS research, and the demo remain unchanged. This new isolated study asks whether directed exploration under uncertain reward estimates yields a better and less failure-prone teacher policy on the same **synthetic**, one-attempt recovery problem. It does not test teacher–student communication or a real robot.

## Controller and rationale

Compare the existing `RpsContextualBandit` (zero action weights, selected-action update `0.03 × (reward − Q) × feature`, epsilon `0.20`) against **one** new ridge LinUCB readout. Both receive the same real-wiring simulated VPN/KC/MBON activity and fixed three-MBON standardization, and both freeze KC→MBON synaptic multipliers. The new controller adds a bias to the three standardized MBON features: `x=[1,f0,f1,f2]`. For each action `a`, initialize `A_a=1.0 I4`, `b_a=0`; predict `mu_a=xᵀ A_a⁻¹ b_a`, uncertainty proxy `s_a=sqrt(xᵀ A_a⁻¹ x)`. During training choose the lowest-index maximizer of `mu_a + 0.5 s_a`. After seeing **only the selected action's** net reward `r`, update `A_a←A_a+xxᵀ`, `b_a←b_a+rx`. Other actions remain unchanged. Frozen decisions maximize `mu_a` with **no uncertainty bonus, exploration, reward update, or neural learning**. The fixed ridge/bonus values are chosen before seeing new results; no sweep or adaptation is allowed. The Gaussian linear confidence proxy measures uncertainty from limited feature/action coverage, not calibrated Bernoulli outcome variance. This can favor under-sampled A2 despite an early unlucky reward sample, but it may still fail if the linear reward model or noisy features are inadequate.

No hidden cause, outcome probability, optimal action, or unchosen potential outcome enters either controller. The runner creates a case's latent cause and three potential outcomes for matched evaluation, passes only its cue through VPN stimulation before the action, and reveals only the selected success/reward afterward. The analytical cue-conditioned best action is used **only for reporting**, not as a controller feature. No RPS controller file, connectome, simulator, dopamine rule, or demo is edited.

## Locked cases, seeds, timing, and measurements

Reuse **exactly** the original recovery task's four equally weighted hidden causes, cue likelihoods, three action success probabilities, delay/escalation penalties, and reward formula. New initial neural identity **15001**, balanced 144-case training tape `rng(2026110821)` and independent 36-case held-out tape `rng(2026110822)`; confirmation identity **15002**, training/evaluation tapes `rng(2026110823/2026110824)`. These identities and case seeds were not found in existing docs/runners. Each tape has 36 training or 9 evaluation cases per cause, with cues and potential outcomes independently drawn and case order shuffled. The prior recovery held-out tape is not reused. The two arms within a stage receive the **same** case order, potential outcomes, neural identity, 400 ms timing (cue 0–240, count reset 50, decision/reward 240, update 340), keyed neural random streams, initial zero reward predictions, and frozen evaluation tape. They differ only in controller learning/exploration.

Checkpoints: **0, 24, 48, 96, 144** training attempts. At each, construct independent frozen simulator/controller instances from the trained state, disable exploration and learning, and evaluate all **36** held-out cases with identical keyed streams. Record trial-level cue, hidden cause for analysis, neural features, predicted means, uncertainty bonuses (training only), action, selected success/reward, failure, delay, escalation, and cause-conditioned expected reward. Preserve full action counts, exploration/coverage, controller matrices/weights, and KC→MBON weight integrity. Distinguish training successes from frozen outcomes. The new controller has no stochastic policy draw; its confidence bonus is the exploration mechanism.

## Preflight and gates

1. **Implementation validation before any neural run:** deterministic no-simulation unit checks must verify positive finite uncertainty, its decrease after a feature/action observation, UCB action selection, selected-action-only matrix/reward updates, fixed neural feature transformation, reproducibility, frozen mean-only selection with unchanged parameters, and no label/cause argument in the controller API. Any failure stops before training.
2. **Matched-run validity:** same training/evaluation case IDs and potential outcomes, identical neural feature vectors per matched case, both controllers initialized at zero mean, exactly 144 own-reward updates per arm, no evaluation updates or uncertainty bonus in frozen decisions, all neural multipliers one, and finite/invertible LinUCB matrices. Failure stops and the run is invalid.
3. **Teacher improvement, initial and confirmation separately:** the LinUCB final frozen **mean cause-conditioned expected net reward ≥0.20**, **≥0.10 over its own initial frozen value**, and **≥0.05 above the matched original controller**. It must achieve **≥22/36 realized recoveries**, select **each action ≥4 times**, and choose A2 on **≥60% of held-out C2 cases**. These are jointly required; selecting A2 alone does not pass.
4. **Learning speed and costly attempts, initial and confirmation separately:** LinUCB's mean frozen expected reward averaged over checkpoints **24/48/96** must exceed the original controller's average by **≥0.04**. Over all **144** training attempts it must incur **at least five fewer failed attempts**, while its **mean realized net reward** must exceed the original by **≥0.03 per attempt**, so extra clearing delay/escalation cannot be hidden by success count alone. Report failed attempts and total delay/escalation at each checkpoint even if the gate fails.

If either teacher or learning-speed gate fails in the initial stage, stop: no seed replacement, confirmation, DN probe, or student training. Run **one** independent confirmation only after every initial gate passes, with the same architecture, thresholds, and budget. If both stages pass, this justifies **designing a separately locked neural receiving and five-arm student study**; it does not itself establish teaching. Write each started stage once to `data/package_recovery_uncertainty_{initial,confirmation}.json`, with the protocol hash and all rows. No tuning on prior or new held-out outcomes.
